# Provider Fraud Anomalies and Behavioral Patterns

## 1. Provider Volume & Frequency Anomalies
- **High Volume Billing:** Providers billing for more than 24 hours of services in a single 24-hour calendar day.
- **Abnormal Claim Clusters:** A high concentration of claims submitted for identical procedure codes within a single week across multiple unrelated patients.
- **Provider Specialty Mismatch:** Billing for specialized procedures (e.g., oncology, neurology) when the registered provider specialty is general practice or physical therapy without certified specialist involvement.

## 2. Discharge & Transfer Anomalies
- **Discharge Type Misrepresentation:** Reporting a discharge status of "Transferred to another facility" or "Rehab/Skilled Nursing" to trigger higher facility payments when the patient was discharged home.
- **Deceased Patient Claims:** Claims for treatments or follow-up procedures allegedly occurring after the official date of patient death.

## 3. Distance & Patient Density Anomalies
- **Provider-Patient Distance:** When a significant portion of a provider's patient base travels over 50 miles for primary routine care, investigate potential patient kickback schemes or identity theft rings.
- **Late Claim Submissions:** Systematic late filing (>90 days past service date) across multiple claims from a single facility often indicates retroactively fabricated treatment records.
