# Healthcare Insurance Fraud Indicators & Red Flags

## 1. Provider Fraud Patterns
### Duplicate Billing
- Submitting multiple claims for the same service, same patient, and same date of service.
- Billing multiple insurance providers for identical treatments without coordination of benefits.
- Splitting single procedures into separate claims to bypass single-claim authorization limits.

### Upcoding & Unbundling
- **Upcoding:** Billing for a higher-level CPT code or more complex treatment than actually rendered (e.g., billing a complex 60-minute evaluation for a 15-minute routine visit).
- **Unbundling:** Billing individual component services separately when a single comprehensive CPT code covers the entire procedure.

### Phantom Billing & Services Not Rendered
- Claims submitted for patients who were never seen by the provider or facilities that do not exist.
- Billing for dead patients or deceased policyholders.

## 2. Patient & Facility Anomalies
### Provider-Patient Geographic Distance
- Claims where the provider and patient are separated by extreme physical distances (>100 miles) for routine outpatient services without logical justification or specialist referral.

### Length of Stay (LOS) Discrepancies
- Outpatient procedure claims reporting inpatient Length of Stay (>0 days).
- Excessively prolonged hospital stays beyond clinical necessity for standard diagnostic procedures.

### High Volume Claim Spikes
- Rapid acceleration in weekly claim volume for a single provider compared to historical peer averages.
- Abnormally high number of claims per patient within a short timeframe.

## 3. Late Submission & Documentation Anomalies
- Claims submitted beyond policy reporting deadlines without prior extension approval.
- Inconsistent discharge codes (e.g., discharge to home vs rehab transfer when procedures indicate minor care).
