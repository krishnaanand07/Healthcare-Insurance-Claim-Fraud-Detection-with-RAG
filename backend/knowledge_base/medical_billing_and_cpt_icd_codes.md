# Medical Billing Codes & Clinical Consistency Standards

## 1. CPT & ICD Code Compatibility
- **ICD-10 (Diagnosis Codes):** Numeric and alphanumeric codes representing medical conditions and patient diagnoses.
- **CPT (Procedure Codes):** 5-digit numeric codes representing medical, surgical, and diagnostic procedures performed by healthcare professionals.
- **Compatibility Rule:** Every billed CPT procedure code must be clinically indicated by the primary or secondary ICD-10 diagnosis code. Unrelated combinations (e.g., billing cardiac surgery for a dermatological rash diagnosis) indicate severe billing errors or intentional fraud.

## 2. Service Types & Admission Standards
- **Inpatient Care:** Involves overnight admission to a hospital or medical facility. Length of stay is expected to be >= 1 day.
- **Outpatient Care:** Diagnostic tests, minor surgeries, or routine medical consultations that do not require an overnight stay. Length of stay MUST be 0 days. Non-zero length of stay on outpatient claims is a strong indicator of upcoding or billing error.
- **Emergency Service:** Immediate medical attention. Admission type is urgent/emergency. High claim frequency or prolonged delay in claim submission for emergency visits warrants verification.

## 3. Financial Metrics & Billing Normalization
- **Deductible Amount:** The amount the patient must pay out-of-pocket before insurance coverage begins.
- **CoPay Amount:** Fixed fee paid by the patient at the time of service.
- **Excessive Claim Amount:** Claims significantly exceeding the regional average reimbursement rate for a given procedure code are flagged for medical review.
